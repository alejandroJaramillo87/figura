figura — hero art-style portfolio

Candidate art styles for post heroes, so a style can be chosen by looking rather than by imagining. Each entry is a real hero built under the real contract — scoped CSS, palette tokens, the is-live / is-settled / is-paused lifecycle, and a complete settled composition for reduced motion.

Serve over HTTP (python3 -m http.server) — the manifest fetch fails over file://. Heroes only autoplay their intro once they scroll into view.

Adding a style: build the hero as kind: hero with post: "showcase" in manifest.json, then add it to a style's pieces in STYLES on this page (a new treatment gets its own entry; another example of an existing one is another pieces item). Nothing else is wired to it.
